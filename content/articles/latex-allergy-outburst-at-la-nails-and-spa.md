*Customer Service Bulletin — Professional Attitude & Medical Allergy Handling Feature*

A single emotional outburst can erase years of customer loyalty. As this case involving **L.A. Nails & Spa** illustrates, failing to respond professionally to a routine medical request can permanently damage trust and overshadow what should have been a memorable family experience.

## A Hysterical Reaction to a Standard Medical Request

According to the customer, she had visited the salon for more than seven years and often returned monthly during the summer. During this visit, she brought her five-year-old daughter for her very first pedicure.

Before the service began, she politely informed the technician that she had a latex allergy and requested non-latex gloves. Instead of calmly accommodating the request, the technician allegedly stood up and shouted across the salon, refusing to perform the service.

## Public Humiliation and a Mother's Shock

The customer said she felt embarrassed and humiliated after being refused service in front of other clients. She emphasized that she had not objected to gloves altogether—only to latex gloves because of a medical allergy.

Management eventually apologized, but only after a noticeable delay. By then, the special experience planned for her daughter had already been overshadowed by the incident. The customer ultimately decided never to return.

## Media Expert's Perspective

This case highlights the importance of medical awareness, emotional intelligence, and professional communication. Allergy notifications should be treated as routine safety information rather than an inconvenience. Immediate managerial intervention, respectful communication, and proper occupational safety training could have preserved a seven-year customer relationship.
