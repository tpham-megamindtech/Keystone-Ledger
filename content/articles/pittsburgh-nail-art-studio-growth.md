Studio Lacquer built its client base almost entirely online before its owner, nail artist Bianca Delgado, opened a physical studio in Pittsburgh's East Liberty neighborhood last year. Delgado had spent three years posting detailed nail art tutorials and time-lapse videos, building a following that translated directly into a fully booked appointment calendar from the studio's opening week.

"I didn't have to build an audience once I opened," Delgado said. "I brought one with me, which is not something most new salons get to do."

The studio specializes in intricate hand-painted designs that take up to two hours per appointment, priced well above standard manicure rates. Delgado says roughly 40 percent of new clients still cite a specific video as the reason they booked, a direct return on the years spent creating free content before monetizing it.

She has hired one additional nail artist and is training a third, though she says maintaining the studio's signature detail level while scaling has required more hands-on training time than she initially expected.
